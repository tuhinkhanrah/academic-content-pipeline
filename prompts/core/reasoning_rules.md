# Reasoning Structure & Solutions

## Complete Solution Requirement
- Every generated question MUST include a complete, self-contained solution.
- The solution must explain the governing concept, identify the correct result, and show enough reasoning for a Class 11/12 learner to follow it.
- Do not provide an answer-only response or skip reasoning because the question appears simple.
- Use only Class 11/12 (NCERT / Pre-Medical / Pre-Engineering) concepts and methods unless the source explicitly requires otherwise.

## Concept-First Explanation
- Explain by concept, value, relationship, or result rather than by answer-option position.
- Do not make the solution depend on option letters, option positions, statement numbers, graph numbers, figure numbers, table rows, or columns.
- State the final conceptual answer clearly after the reasoning.

## Numerical, Algebraic, and Diagram Questions
Use these five stages in order:
1. **Given Data and Conditions**: List every given value, symbol, label, unit, condition, and constraint. For a diagram, describe the layout being interpreted.
2. **Assumptions and Sign Conventions**: State the physical or mathematical model, assumptions, directions, signs, reference points, and diagram interpretation.
3. **Relevant Formulae and Principles**: Write every equation, formula, theorem, or principle used.
4. **Step-by-Step Working**: Show substitutions and transformations line by line. Include units and signs. Do not skip algebraic logic or intermediate values.
5. **Check and Final Answer**: Check units, bounds, signs, limiting behavior, or physical plausibility, then state the final answer clearly.

## Conceptual and Qualitative Questions
- Use sequential steps such as Conceptual Principle, Evaluation, Application, and Final Conclusion as appropriate.
- Connect each conclusion to the stated scientific principle or evidence.
- Address why the correct concept applies without referring only to an option label or position.

## Bilingual and Multilingual Solutions
- When more than one target language is requested, provide the complete English solution first and then the complete translated solution.
- Translate every step, intermediate explanation, unit interpretation, and final conclusion.
- Do not shorten or omit the translated reasoning.
- Keep mathematical expressions, symbols, and units language-agnostic where appropriate, without duplicating them unnecessarily.

## Final Reasoning Check
Before returning each question, verify that:
1. A complete solution is present.
2. The required reasoning stages are present for numerical, algebraic, and diagram questions.
3. No intermediate calculation or stated constraint needed to justify the answer is missing.
4. The explanation is concept-based rather than position-based.
5. The bilingual solution is complete when multiple languages are requested.
