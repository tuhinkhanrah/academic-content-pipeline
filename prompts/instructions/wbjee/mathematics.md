# Examination Instructions: Subject - MATHEMATICS

* **Duration:** 2 Hours
* **Full Marks:** 100
* **Important Note:** DO NOT OPEN THIS BOOKLET UNTIL YOU ARE ASKED TO DO SO.

---

### 📝 General Guidelines
* **Question Type:** All questions are objective type with four answer options for each.
* **Rough Work:** Must be done strictly inside the Question Booklet on the designated blank pages.
* **Submission:** Hand over the completed OMR sheet to the invigilator before leaving.
* **Language Discrepancy:** Questions are provided in English and Bengali. If any discrepancy exists, the **English version** will be treated as final.
* **Booklet Retention:** Candidates are allowed to take the Question Booklet with them after the examination is over.

---

### 📊 Question Categories & Marking Schemes

#### Category-1
* Category-1 (Q.1 to Q.50)
* Carries **1 mark** each.
* Only **one** option is correct.
* Incorrect answer or multiple options selected: **-¼ mark** deducted.

#### Category-2
* Category-2 (Q.51 to Q.65)
* Carries **2 marks** each.
* Only **one** option is correct.
* Incorrect answer or multiple options selected: **-½ mark** deducted.

#### Category-3
* Category-3 (Q.66 to Q.75)
* One or more options may be correct.
* **Full Marks (+2):** Awarded only if all correct options are selected.
* **Zero Marks (0):** Awarded if any incorrect option is selected, even if correct options are also marked.
* **Partial Marks:** Awarded if only correct options are marked, but not all of them. Calculated using the formula:
  $$\text{Marks} = \frac{2 \times (\text{Number of correct options marked})}{\text{Total number of correct options}}$$
* **Unattempted:** Fetch zero marks.

---

### 🖊️ OMR Sheet Instructions
* **Bubbling:** Use **only Black or Blue ink ballpoint pen** to fill the bubbles completely (A, B, C, or D).
* **Markings:** Do not put any stray marks or extra markings anywhere else on the OMR Sheet.
* **Details Required:** Write and bubble your Question Booklet number and Roll Number carefully.
* **Identity:** Write your name in block letters, examination center, and signature exactly as they appear on your Admit Card.
* **Invalidation:** Errors in filling bubbles, discrepancy in signatures, folding, or tearing the sheet will make the OMR Sheet invalid. The candidate bears sole responsibility.

---

### 🚫 Prohibited Items & Code of Conduct
* **Banned Items:** Candidates are **not allowed** to carry written/printed material, calculators, slide rules, pens, log-tables, wristwatches, graphs, or communication devices (mobile phones, Bluetooth devices, etc.) into the hall.
* **Penalty:** Any candidate found with prohibited items will be reported against, and their candidature will be summarily cancelled.
